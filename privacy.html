Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 September 2026

The short version
  • We store only what Jarvis needs to work: your account details and the reminders, notes and updates you create.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell or share your data for marketing.
  • Jarvis’s AI runs on your own device. What you ask it is not sent to us or to any AI company.
  • You can export everything or permanently delete your account at any time, inside the app.

1. Who we are

Jarvis is provided by Carean Moyo (“we”, “us”), based in the United Kingdom. We are the data controller for personal data processed through Jarvis. Contact us about privacy at careanmoyo1@gmail.com.

What we don’t collect

We don’t use advertising, analytics, fingerprinting or third-party tracking, and we don’t access your contacts, photos, location, calendar or microphone.

3. Our legal basis (UK GDPR)

  • Contract: to provide the service you signed up for (your account, content, sync and notifications).
  • Legitimate interests: to keep Jarvis secure and working, for example by preventing abuse and fixing faults.
  • Consent: for notifications. You choose to turn them on and can turn them off at any time in Settings or in your device settings.

4. Who we share data with

We never sell your data. We use a small number of service providers (“processors”) who handle data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and account emails. Your data is stored in the EU (Paris).
  • Vercel: hosts the Jarvis website and app files.
  • Push notification services run by Apple, Google or Mozilla, depending on your device: they deliver notifications. Notification content is end-to-end encrypted for your device, so these services can’t read it.
  • Hugging Face: if you choose to download an on-device AI model, it’s downloaded from their public servers, which see your IP address like any website. No personal content is sent.

We may disclose data if required by law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users or others.

5. International transfers

Your account data is stored in the EU. Some providers (such as Vercel) are based in the United States. Where data is transferred outside the UK or EU, it’s protected by approved safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account and content: until you delete them or delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, your data is permanently erased from our database straight away. Any copies in our providers’ backups or logs are removed on their normal schedules.
  • Notification subscriptions are removed when you turn notifications off, sign out, or the device stops accepting them.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to receive a copy of it (portability). In the app:

  • Export: Settings › Your data › Export backup.
  • Correct: edit anything directly, or your details in Settings.
  • Delete: Settings › Delete Account permanently erases your account and everything in it. See also how to delete your account.

For anything else, email careanmoyo1@gmail.com. We’ll respond within one month. If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

8. Cookies and local storage

Jarvis doesn’t use cookies for tracking or advertising. It uses your browser’s local storage only for things that are strictly necessary for the app to work: keeping you signed in, your settings, an offline copy of your data, and the on-device AI model. Because this storage is essential to the service you asked for, no cookie consent banner is required.

9. Security

All connections use HTTPS. Each person’s data is isolated at the database level, so accounts can only ever access their own data. Passwords are stored as secure hashes, and notification content is end-to-end encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

10. Children

Jarvis isn’t intended for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

11. Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we’ll update the date above and let you know in the app before they take effect.
