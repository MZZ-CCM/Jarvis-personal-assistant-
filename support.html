Signing in

I didn’t get my confirmation or password-reset email

Check your spam folder. We can send only a limited number of account emails each hour, so if it still hasn’t arrived, wait a little and use Resend confirmation email on the sign-in screen, or try Forgot password again later.

It says “Try again in…”

After several attempts, Jarvis pauses sign-in briefly to protect your account. Wait for the countdown, then try again.

Notifications

How do I get reminders when Jarvis is closed?

Open Settings › Notifications and tap Turn On next to “When Jarvis is closed”. On iPhone, add Jarvis to your Home Screen first (Safari › Share › Add to Home Screen), open it from there, and then turn notifications on.

I’m not getting notifications

Make sure notifications are allowed for Jarvis in your device settings and that Focus / Do Not Disturb isn’t blocking them. Then use Send a test notification in Settings.

Your data

How do I export my data?

Settings › Your data › Export backup downloads all your reminders, notes and updates as a file.

Deleting your account

In the app (instant): open Jarvis › Settings › Delete Account, type DELETE and confirm. Your account and all its data are permanently erased straight away.

Without the app: email careanmoyo1@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Jarvis account”. We’ll confirm and delete your account and all associated data within 7 days.

What’s deleted: your account, name, email, settings, reminders, notes, updates and notification subscriptions. Nothing is kept afterwards, except where the law requires it, and copies in our providers’ backups and logs are removed on their normal schedules.
